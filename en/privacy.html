Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Wekker, published by Jelle Coremans, Aannemersstraat 166, 9040 Ghent, Belgium (company/VAT number: BE0636677514). In short: Wekker collects no personal data and sends nothing to us.

What data does the app use?

  • Your alarms and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • Approximate location: only if you allow it, the app uses your approximate location to calculate sunrise and sunset times. This location stays on your device and is never transmitted.
  • Light and proximity sensors: while an alarm is ringing, the app measures ambient light to dim the screen in a dark room. These readings are not stored.
  • Export and import: if you export your alarms, you choose where the file is saved.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking services.
  • The app has no internet access, so it sends no data to us or to third parties.

Android backup

If you have enabled Android's automatic backup, Android may include the app's settings (including your alarms and the stored location) in your own Google backup. That backup is managed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Purchases

If you buy the Pro version, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you have bought Pro. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request or delete. You can remove all of the app's data by uninstalling it or via Settings → Apps → Wekker → Storage → Clear data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
