Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Wekko, published by Jelle Coremans (trading as Fixwell), Aannemersstraat 166, 9040 Ghent, Belgium (company/VAT number: BE0636677514). In short: Wekko sends nothing to us. Only for features you switch on yourself does the app connect to an external service.

What data does the app use?

  • Your alarms and settings are stored only on your own device.
  • Approximate location: only if you allow it, the app uses your approximate location to calculate sunrise and sunset times. This location is stored on your device. If you switch on the weather on the alarm screen, the app sends this location (rounded) to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast; see below.
  • Light and proximity sensors: while an alarm is ringing, the app measures ambient light to dim the screen in a dark room. These readings are not stored.
  • Export and import: if you export your alarms, you choose where the file is saved.

Internet connections

The app only connects to the internet for features you choose yourself:

  • Weather on the alarm screen (off by default): the app requests the forecast from Open-Meteo using your rounded location. No account, name or device ID is sent. See the Open-Meteo privacy policy.
  • Internet radio as alarm sound: station search uses the open station list of radio-browser.info; the station you choose is played directly from that station.
  • Resume music app: the app only tells your own music app on the device to "play"; Wekko itself sends nothing.

As with any internet connection, these services see your IP address. We receive none of this data.

What the app does not do

  • No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking services.
  • The app sends no data to us and sells or shares nothing for advertising.

Android backup

If you have enabled Android's automatic backup, Android may include the app's settings (including your alarms and the stored location) in your own Google backup. That backup is managed by Google under Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Purchases

If you buy the Pro version, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We receive no payment details; the app only asks Google Play whether you have bought Pro. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to request or delete. You can remove all of the app's data by uninstalling it or via Settings → Apps → Wekko → Storage → Clear data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
