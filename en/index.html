Wekko

A calm alarm clock app for Android.

Wekko makes waking up simple and pleasant:

  • Alarms and groups of alarms, set per day
  • Colours that follow sunrise and sunset
  • Dimmed alarm screen in a dark room
  • Snooze or stop straight from the notification
  • Widgets to switch an alarm or group on or off from your home screen
  • No ads, no tracking, no account needed
