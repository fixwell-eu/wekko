Wekko

Alarms in groups, on or off with one tap.

Work week, weekend, early shift, holiday: with Wekko you put your alarms in groups and switch a whole group on or off at once. No more fiddling with ten separate alarms.

📁 Groups

  • Put alarms in groups, each with its own name and icon
  • One switch for the whole group; partly on shows at a glance
  • Drag alarms between groups or into your own order
  • Duplicate a group to make a variation quickly

👆 Convenience

  • Widgets: switch an alarm or group on or off from your home screen
  • See right away how long you can still sleep
  • Skip just the next time, without turning anything off
  • Snooze or stop from the notification, even while using your phone

🌅 Wake up calmly

  • Sound that starts softly and slowly gets louder
  • A big snooze button, or tap anywhere on the screen
  • The screen dims by itself in a dark bedroom

Free and Pro

Free with up to 3 alarms, without ads. With Wekko Pro you can make as many as you like: €10 forever or €2 a year.
